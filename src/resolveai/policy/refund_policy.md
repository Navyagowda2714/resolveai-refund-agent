# Refund & Returns Policy (v2026.10)

## POL-1.1 Return windows
Customers may request a refund within the return window, counted in days from the delivery date.
The default window is 30 days. Apparel has an extended 60-day window. Beauty products have a 14-day
window and grocery items a 7-day window because of hygiene and freshness constraints.

## POL-1.2 Proof of purchase
A refund requires a matching order on the customer's account. Requests referencing an order that
belongs to a different account must be verified by a human agent before any money moves.

## POL-2.2 Final sale items
Items marked final sale (clearance) cannot be returned for change of mind. Final sale items that
arrive damaged, defective, wrong, missing or never arrive remain eligible for a refund.

## POL-2.3 Non-refundable categories
Gift cards and digital downloads such as software licences are non-refundable once issued or
delivered.

## POL-3.1 Damaged on arrival
Items damaged in transit are refunded in full for the affected items. A photo of the damage is
requested but not mandatory for low-value items. The customer does not need to return the item.

## POL-3.2 Defective items and warranty
Items that stop working or are faulty are refunded in full within the return window. Defects
reported after the return window but within 365 days of delivery are handled as warranty claims by
a human agent, who may offer repair, replacement or refund.

## POL-3.3 Wrong item received
If the customer receives a different item than ordered, the ordered item is refunded in full and a
prepaid return label is issued for the wrong item.

## POL-3.4 Missing items
If an item is missing from a multi-item order, only the missing item is refunded. Shipping is not
refunded when the rest of the order was delivered.

## POL-4.1 Order not received
If an order was not delivered and the carrier has no delivery confirmation, the full order amount
including shipping is refunded. If the carrier confirms delivery, the case becomes a carrier
dispute and must be investigated by a human agent.

## POL-4.2 Orders still in transit
Orders that have shipped and are within 10 days of the order date are considered in transit. Refund
requests for non-receipt are declined until the grace period has passed; the customer is given
tracking information instead.

## POL-4.3 Late delivery compensation
If an order arrives later than promised, the shipping fee is refunded. If shipping was free, a
goodwill credit of 10 percent of the item value is offered instead.

## POL-5.1 Change of mind
Change-of-mind returns within the window are refunded minus a 15 percent restocking fee, provided
the item is unused and not final sale.

## POL-6.1 Duplicate requests
An order that has already been refunded in full cannot be refunded again. Partial prior refunds
reduce the remaining refundable amount.

## POL-7.1 Mandatory human review
Tickets mentioning legal action, a bank chargeback or dispute, or a safety hazard such as burns,
fire, smoke or injury must always be reviewed by a human agent.

## POL-7.2 Unauthorized charges
Reports of charges the customer does not recognise are potential account takeover and are routed
to the trust and safety team. No automated refund is issued.

## POL-8.1 Unclear requests
When the issue cannot be determined from the request, a human agent contacts the customer for
clarification.

## POL-9.1 Automation limits
Automated refunds are limited to 150 EUR per ticket and to customers with a low fraud risk score.
Anything above the limit or with elevated risk is reviewed by a human agent with the AI assessment
attached.
